# Cancellation Policy

> Synthetic policy for a fictional demo store; not the policy of any real company.

Cancellation applies to a whole order, not to individual items.

## CAN-5.1 Orders may be cancelled before shipment

An order can be cancelled while it has been placed but not yet handed to a carrier. When an
order is cancelled, its entire captured payment, including shipping, is reversed. That
reversal is part of the cancellation itself, not a refund request under the Refund Policy.

## CAN-5.2 Shipped or delivered orders cannot be cancelled

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, whether it is still in transit or
has been delivered. After delivery, the customer may instead use the Returns Policy or the
Damaged Items Policy.

## CAN-5.3 Cancelled orders cannot be cancelled again

An order that is already cancelled cannot be cancelled a second time.
