# Damaged Items Policy

> Synthetic policy for a fictional demo store; not the policy of any real company.

This policy covers items that arrive damaged or defective. A damaged item may be refunded,
returned, or (subject to the Replacement Policy) replaced.

## DMG-4.1 Damage reported within 30 days of delivery

Damage must be reported within 30 days of delivery. Days are counted exactly as in the
Returns Policy: UTC calendar days from the delivery date, where day 30 is the last eligible
day and day 31 is outside the damage-reporting window, regardless of the time of delivery.

## DMG-4.2 Damaged items are eligible even if non-returnable

A damaged item reported within the window is eligible even when the product is marked
non-returnable. Non-returnable status only prevents change-of-mind requests (RET-3.2). An
eligible damaged-item refund also includes the unrefunded shipping charge (RFD-2.2).
