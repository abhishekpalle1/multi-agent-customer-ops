# Replacement Policy

> Synthetic policy for a fictional demo store; not the policy of any real company.

A replacement ships a new unit of the same product in place of a delivered one. It must also
satisfy the Damaged Items Policy (DMG-4.1 and DMG-4.2).

## RPL-6.1 Replacements are only for damaged items

Replacements are offered only for damaged items. A change-of-mind request is never fulfilled
as a replacement, even when the product is in stock.

## RPL-6.2 Replacements require available stock

A replacement is possible only when the product's available inventory covers the requested
quantity. If it does not, the damaged item is not eligible for a replacement, although it may
still be eligible for a refund under the Damaged Items Policy.
